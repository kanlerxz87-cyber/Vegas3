Activities with confirmed schedules or dates

  • Fountains of Bellagio: free. The official schedule lists weekday shows from 3 p.m. and weekend/holiday shows from noon, then every 15 minutes from 8 p.m. to midnight; weather can cancel the outdoor display.
  • Bellagio Conservatory: the official 2027 calendar places the Lunar New Year installation from 9 January to 6 March, which includes 15–18 February. Display hours and admission price were not stated in the captured page.
  • Tournament of Kings: on-property at Excalibur; the current official page lists Wednesday–Monday shows at 6 p.m. and 8:30 p.m. and a $74.40 ticket before tax. Feb. 14, 2027 is listed as a dark date; exact Feb. 15 show inventory still needs a direct ticket check.
  • Viva Vision at Fremont Street Experience: free hourly light shows from 6 p.m. through 2 a.m. nightly. Exact one-hour bus time from each candidate hotel was not verified.
  • The Mob Museum / High Roller / Sphere: official hours or ticket information are linked on the activities page; date-specific changes and RTC travel times still need checking.
