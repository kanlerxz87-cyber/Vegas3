What to do, and what transit is verified

The RTC system is bus-based. On-property activities are verified only for the matching hotel; other options stay in a separate candidate list until an RTC Trip Planner itinerary confirms a 60-minute or shorter trip.

Transit limitation: the Deuce map confirms the Strip and downtown corridor, not door-to-door time. No one-hour travel claim is made for an activity away from the matching hotel. RTC says 15 Feb 2027 uses Saturday route schedules for Presidents Day. View RTC schedules ↗
