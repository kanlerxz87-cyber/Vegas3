### Direct room-price checks

No usable official 7-night total was returned, so every hotel total and “cheapest time to book” cell is intentionally blank.

- **Caesars Palace:** the official February 15–22, 2027 rate-calendar request returned “Sorry! Our website is currently experiencing technical difficulties.” A second February 1–8 query returned the same. The hotel page lists an advanced-purchase offer of up to 30% off for stays booked 30+ days ahead, but that does not prove the offer is available for these dates or is the lowest rate. The official booking page showed a $62.30/night resort fee including tax; for seven nights, that fee alone is $436.10 (7 × $62.30), **not** the room total.
- **Excalibur:** the official booking engine accepted February 15–22, 2027 and two guests, but returned “Sorry, there was an error loading calendar dates pricing.” The hotel homepage displayed “up to 40% off Room Rates + $50 F&B Credit” (up to 55% for members) on the check date; no date-specific total or offer end date was returned.
- **The Venetian:** the official booking calendar accepted February 15–22, 2027, but the captured calendar displayed `$XXX` placeholders rather than usable nightly rates. It states that the displayed best-available suite rate includes the daily resort fee but excludes taxes. No total could be verified.
- For MGM Grand, Cosmopolitan and Bellagio, official room pages provided room details and rate links, but no date-specific price was captured during this research pass. They are not assigned estimated rates.

**Interpretation:** February dates are recognized by the booking interfaces; the evidence does not establish that the dates are unbookable or that the booking window is closed. It establishes only that a real, date-specific quote could not be verified from the accessible official results on 26 November 2026. No “cheapest booking date” is asserted without comparable, date-specific totals.

## RTC public transit and one-hour counts

- [RTC Trip Planner](https://www.rtcsnv.com/tripplanner/) is the official origin/destination/date/time route tool. Its captured page exposes the form but not a route result for the candidate hotel-to-restaurant pairs.
- The official [RTC Deuce schedule and map](https://rtcws.rtcsnv.com/routepdf/deuce.pdf) shows the Deuce operating 24 hours daily; approximately every 10–15 minutes from 7 a.m.–1 a.m. and every 20 minutes from 1 a.m.–7 a.m. It lists the Strip hotel corridor (including Excalibur, MGM Grand, Cosmopolitan/Bellagio, Caesars Palace and Venetian) and downtown stops. The schedule notes that frequencies are approximate and traffic/construction can cause delays.
- RTC is a bus system; there is no RTC subway. The Las Vegas Monorail is a separate service and is not counted as RTC bus travel.
- RTC’s official [Schedules & Maps](https://www.rtcsnv.com/ways-to-travel/schedules-maps/) page says that on **15 February 2027 (Presidents Day)** RTC will operate Saturday route schedules. This is important for the preferred arrival day and the 7-night comparison week.
- The on-property counts above are lower bounds. A full one-hour count and all hotel-to-activity travel-time cells remain blank until each itinerary can be checked in RTC’s trip planner for a specified date/time. No subway travel or unverified travel duration is inferred.
