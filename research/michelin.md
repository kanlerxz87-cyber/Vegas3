## Verified source notes and irregularities

- The LVCVA page’s FAQ gives the four starred restaurants and the three Bib Gourmand restaurants; the MGM venue pages independently show the 2026 distinction for the three MGM-run Las Vegas entries. Caesars’ Guy Savoy page states it has a Michelin star; the LVCVA page supplies the current 2026 distinction.
- COTE has a Michelin-starred New York brand/location, but the **Las Vegas** row on the 2026 LVCVA list is Recommended. Do not label COTE Las Vegas as starred.
- The 2026 list places Wing Lei under Recommended. The Wynn page also describes older awards/history; no historical star is carried into the current Las Vegas status.
- MGM’s current Joël Robuchon detail page says **Two Stars, 2026**, while the restaurant’s Instagram profile bio still says “Michelin 3-Star.” Use the current 2026 venue page and LVCVA selection for the table; flag the social-bio mismatch for review.
- The official Bellagio page marks Michael Mina **temporarily closed** as of the research check. Its 2026 Guide selection is preserved, but do not treat it as bookable for February 2027 until the venue confirms reopening.
- The LVCVA page labels Lamaii “Off-Strip”; Lamaii’s current Chinatown location page gives 4480 Spring Mountain Road. Preserve the LVCVA category and the venue address rather than silently choosing one geography label.
- The LVCVA’s Kame Omakase link is `kameomakase.com`; the fetched link resolves to a page titled Kame Omakase hosted at `sushikame.com`. Use the current site link for contact details and flag the redirect/brand-domain change.
