## Verification and review notes

- **China Poblano hours:** an earlier capture showed 10 a.m.–10:30 p.m. daily. The current official MGM detail page now lists the weekday/weekend schedule above. The site uses the current detail page and flags future holiday exceptions for recheck.
- **Yen Viet Kitchen hours conflict:** its official homepage fetch did not expose hours. A search excerpt from that same official domain contained both “Mon–Sun: 10 AM–9 PM” and “Tue: Closed.” Because the official text conflicts, the table leaves hours blank rather than selecting one version. The issue is on Flagged for Review.
- **Michael Mina:** the official Bellagio page labels it temporarily closed; the 2026 guide distinction remains in the MICHELIN inventory, but the restaurant is not recommended for a February reservation until reopening is confirmed.
- **Joël Robuchon social bio:** the Instagram profile says “Michelin 3-Star,” while current 2026 LVCVA/MGM pages say 2 Stars. The social link is provided for reference only; the 2026 distinction is sourced from the current guide/venue pages.
- **Guy Savoy, Joël Robuchon, L’Atelier, é:** menu prices were not posted in accessible current official pages for full dinners. Guy Savoy’s source lists signature a-la-carte items, so only an entrée-pair example is shown.
- **COTE:** the Las Vegas selection is Recommended, even though the brand’s New York location is starred. The Las Vegas row is not labeled as starred.
- **Michael Mina:** the official page’s seasonal closure notice is for late 2026. A 2027 reopening schedule was not available.
- **Bouchon/COTE/Milos/Mott 32:** detailed recurring hours were present on Venetian pages; seasonal/holiday modifications for February 2027 were not published in the captured sources.
- **Unpriced restaurants:** blank costs are not estimates. Each blank price needs an official current menu/tasting price before it can be included in a final dining budget.
- **Socials:** link only profiles that could be matched to the restaurant/brand; blank cells are not filled with a generic hotel account. A social handle is not treated as evidence of a MICHELIN rating.
