## RTC facts affecting the proposed dates

- [RTC Trip Planner](https://www.rtcsnv.com/tripplanner/) asks for origin, destination, date and time, but the captured response did not include a route result. Exact times are therefore blank rather than inferred.
- The official [Deuce map/schedule](https://rtcws.rtcsnv.com/routepdf/deuce.pdf) states 24-hour daily operation; approximate frequency is every 10–15 minutes 7 a.m.–1 a.m. and about every 20 minutes 1 a.m.–7 a.m. It includes Strip properties and downtown/Fremont stops. The schedule warns that traffic, construction and disruptions may cause delays.
- RTC’s official [Schedules & Maps](https://www.rtcsnv.com/ways-to-travel/schedules-maps/) page lists Saturday route schedules on Presidents Day, **15 February 2027**. Do not use a regular Monday schedule for that date.
- A trip time from a hotel lobby to a destination includes access to the bus stop, waiting, ride time, transfers and destination walk. None of those totals is inferred here.
