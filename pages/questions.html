QuestionAnswer
Are the requested dates a federal holiday?Feb 15, 2027 is Washington's Birthday (Presidents' Day), a federal holiday. Source S14.
Which Las Vegas restaurants currently hold Michelin stars?Four in the 2026 guide: Joël Robuchon (Two Stars); L'Atelier de Joël Robuchon, Restaurant Guy Savoy, é by José Andrés (One Star each). Source S1.
Is there a subway in Las Vegas?Not found in the official RTC material reviewed. RTC describes bus service (S15). Treat the 1-hour test as bus-only until a source says otherwise.
Can I get to Red Rock by bus?No. BLM states public transportation does not extend to Red Rock Canyon (S16).
When do starred restaurants open reservations?Most release tables 30 to 60 days ahead (S1). é by José Andrés allows online booking up to three months ahead (S4).
